* Game 21: Blue wins in 7 turns
:PROPERTIES:
:STEPS: nk|SSE|wk|NN|nk|FE|wk|FS|sfsfef|FS|ek|FS|efn|F
:WINNER: blue
:TURNS: 7
:END:

** Play-by-play
- Turn 1 (Red): =nk= — Red moves north and banks, gaining only 1 territory while saving a move.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =SSE= — Blue spends banked move for 3 actions, expanding south and east to claim 3 territory.
- Turn 2 (Red): =wk= — Red moves west and banks again, now 2 territory but 2 banked moves.
- Turn 2 (Blue): =NN= — Blue pushes north, reaching 5 territory. Fort-eligible!
- Turn 3 (Red): =nk= — Red banks a third time, slowly climbing to 3 territory.
- Turn 3 (Blue): =FE= — Blue fortifies (fort 1) and moves east. Blue begins fort building.
- Turn 4 (Red): =wk= — Fourth bank by Red. 4 territory, 4 banked, but 0 fort supply remaining.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =FS= — Blue places fort 2 and moves south, continuing the fort chain.
- Turn 5 (Red): =sfsfef= — Red finally unleashes banked moves in a 6-action burst: move, fort, move, fort, move, fort. 3 forts placed in one explosive turn!
- Turn 5 (Blue): =FS= — Blue calmly places fort 3.
- Turn 6 (Red): =ek= — Red moves east and banks. Still only 3 forts.
- Turn 6 (Blue): =FS= — Blue places fort 4. One more needed.
- Turn 7 (Red): =efn= — Red expands but cannot keep up.
- Turn 7 (Blue): =F= — Blue places fort 5. Blue wins!

** Key moment
Red banked 4 times in the first 4 turns, building to a massive Turn 5 burst of 3 forts. But Blue's steady fortification pace of one fort per turn starting from Turn 3 was too fast -- Blue finished first despite Red's explosive play.

** Lesson
Heavy banking delays territory growth, giving the opponent a head start. Even a burst turn cannot overcome a 3-turn head start in fortification.

** Strategy Notes

- *Banking*: Delays territory growth but enables burst plays later. Best used sparingly (1-2 banks) rather than heavily (4+ banks).
- *Expansion*: Consistent territory gain provides a strong foundation for forts. The most reliable baseline strategy.
- *Aggressive*: Disrupts opponent but can leave own territory thin. Most effective when paired with fort focus.
- *Defensive*: Safe territory building but cedes initiative. In symmetric defense, Red's first-move advantage is decisive.
- *Fort-focused*: Best overall strategy. Always fortify when possible, expand only when necessary.
- *First-mover advantage*: Red won 14/20 games (70%). Blue's compensating banked move is significant but not fully equalizing.
- *Average game length*: 7.5 turns. Shortest games (7 turns) come from focused play; longest (9 turns) from random or aggressive play.
