#+TITLE: Terratri Games -- Batch 3 (Games 41-60)

* Summary

20 games played exploring counter-strategies, tempo, spatial patterns, and endgame technique.
Red won 9/20, Blue won 11/20. Blue's surprisingly strong showing came from counter-strategies
that exploit Red's predictable openings, and from banking-burst tactics that overcome the
first-mover disadvantage.

- Turn 1 (Red): =nn= -- Aggressive center push c1->c2->c3. 2 territory.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =EES= -- Counter-flanks away from Red: c5->d5->e5->e4. Uses banked move. 3 territory.

- Turn 3 (Blue): =FS= -- Fortifies e2, south to e1. Blue's first fort.

- Turn 5 (Red): =nn= -- North to d3, north to d4. Repositioning.
- Turn 5 (Blue): =FW= -- Fortifies d1, west to c1. 3 forts. Blue claims Red's starting square!

Blue's counter-aggressive flanking to the east edge (EES opening) completely avoided Red's

** Lesson

claimed unchallenged territory and built a fort chain that Red could not disrupt.
