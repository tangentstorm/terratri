#+TITLE: Terratri Games -- Batch 5 (Games 81-100)

* Summary

20 games played with various strategy matchups. Red won 16/20, Blue won 4/20.
Red's first-mover advantage is significant but can be overcome by the BlueOpt
strategy which banks early to fuel a territory burst on turn 2, then fortifies
aggressively from below.

* Game 81: Red wins in 7 turns
:PROPERTIES:
:STEPS: nn|SES|wn|SE|wf|FN|nf|FN|ef|FN|ef|WF|sf|
:WINNER: red
:TURNS: 7
:RED_STRATEGY: Smart
:BLUE_STRATEGY: Smart
:END:

** Play-by-play
- Turn 1 (Red): =nn= -- Pushes straight up the center column c1->c2->c3, claiming 2 territory.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =SES= -- Uses banked move for 3 actions: south to c4, east to d4, south to d3. Claims 3 territory.
- Turn 2 (Red): =wn= -- Flanks west then north, c3->b3->b4. Red has 4 territory now.
- Turn 2 (Blue): =SE= -- Continues southeast, d3->d2->e2. Blue reaches 5 territory.
- Turn 3 (Red): =wf= -- Moves to a4 and places first fort. Red now at 5 territory, 1 fort.
- Turn 3 (Blue): =FN= -- Fortifies at e2, then north to e3. Blue matches with 1 fort.
- Turn 4 (Red): =nf= -- North to a5, fortify. 2 forts.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =FN= -- Fortify at e3, north to e4. 2 forts.
- Turn 5 (Red): =ef= -- East to b5, fortify. 3 forts.
- Turn 5 (Blue): =FN= -- Fortify at e4, north to e5. 3 forts.
- Turn 6 (Red): =ef= -- East to c5, fortify. 4 forts. Red claims Blue's old territory.
- Turn 6 (Blue): =WF= -- West to d5, fortify. 4 forts.
- Turn 7 (Red): =sf= -- South to c4, fortify. 5 forts. RED WINS.

** Key moment
Turn 3 was decisive. Red reached the west edge first and began fortifying along the top row,
while Blue was stuck on the east side. Red's westward expansion gave access to the entire
top row for a fort chain.

** Lesson
When both sides play the same smart strategy, Red's tempo advantage (moving first) translates
to reaching fortification threshold one turn earlier, which cascades into victory.

* Tournament Analysis

** Strategy Rankings (by win rate)
1. *BlueOpt* -- Won all games it played (4/4 as Blue, 1/1 as Red). Bank-burst-through is dominant.
2. *Smart* -- Strong all-around. Wins most Red matchups (5/7). Loses only to BlueOpt.
3. *Rush* -- Decent. Wins as Red (4/5) but loses to BlueOpt.
4. *Edge/Wall/Zigzag* -- All viable as Red but converge to similar outcomes.
5. *AllBank/Passive* -- Banking works but no better than Smart. Same win timeline.
6. *Expand* -- Equivalent to Smart in practice.
7. *Defensive* -- Worst strategy. Loses even as Red to aggressive Blue.
8. *Center* -- Catastrophically bad. Oscillation trap prevents fortification entirely.

** Key Findings

1. *First-mover advantage is strong but not absolute.* Red won 16/20 games (80%), but
   BlueOpt proves Blue can win consistently with the right approach.

2. *BlueOpt is the best strategy found.* Its bank-then-burst pattern exploits a fundamental
   weakness: early territory is unfortified and vulnerable to being overwritten.

3. *Banking is powerful when timed correctly.* Banking early creates burst turns of 3-5
   actions that can catch up from behind. But banking too much (AllBank) wastes tempo.

4. *Oscillation is the worst failure mode.* The Center strategy demonstrates that greedy
   movement without memory leads to infinite loops. Strategies must track territory.

5. *Fort placement path matters more than fort timing.* Smart beats Rush because it finds
   more efficient paths to place 5 forts, even though Rush tries to fortify sooner.

6. *Games are short.* Average game length is 7.7 turns. The longest games (10 turns) involve
   Rush or Random strategies that waste moves repositioning.
