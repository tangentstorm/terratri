* Research Findings

** Optimal Red fort path?
The row-5 sweep (fort along top row through Blue-abandoned territory) and the
adaptive L-shape are tied for fastest. Both achieve 7-turn wins against BlueOpt.
The key principle is self-fueling: fort squares should be on enemy/unclaimed
territory, not Red own dots. Column sweeps along Red expansion axis are slowest
because they consume Red dots (self-consuming pattern).

** Optimal Blue fort path?
Blue mirror of the row sweep (bottom-row sweep through Red territory) is fastest
when accessible. Adaptive play with BlueOpt is the most reliable. The FEF
double-fort remains Blue best technique for overcoming first-mover disadvantage.

** Does blocking with forts work?
Blocking is a viable secondary strategy but not a primary win condition. The
tempo cost of moving toward the opponent for blocking usually exceeds the
disruption benefit. The one exception is the c4 block, which specifically

** Can a player come back from 2 forts behind?
No, not against competent play. 2 forts behind is approximately 3-4 tempi,
which cannot be recovered. The maximum recoverable deficit is 1 fort, achievable
only through aggressive territory invasion (destroying opponent dots below the
5-territory threshold). Symmetric handicaps preserve first-mover advantage.

** Fort placement principles (revised)
1. Self-fueling > self-consuming: Fort on enemy/unclaimed territory preserves
   your dot count. Fort on own territory reduces dots, forcing repositioning.
2. Straight-line > diagonal: Fort-move-fort in one direction is 2 actions per
   fort. Diagonal requires alternating, costing 3+ actions per fort.
3. Row sweep > column sweep: Rows cross both players territory; columns run
   parallel to expansion axes and tend to self-consume.
4. Adaptive > forced geometry: Smart evaluation naturally picks high-value
   fort squares. Forced geometric patterns often hit territory threshold issues.
5. Connected forts (walls) are powerful but expensive: A 3-fort wall blocks
   opponent movement but costs 3 turns. Only worth it if opponent has a single
   fort lane that can be cut.
