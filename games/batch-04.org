#+TITLE: Terratri Games -- Batch 4 (Games 61-80)

* Game 61: Red wins in 8 turns
:PROPERTIES:
:STEPS: en|SWS|nw|SE|wn|WS|fx|WN|sf|FX|sf|NX|sf|NX|wf
:WINNER: red
:TURNS: 8
:END:

** Play-by-play
- Turn 1 (Red): =en= -- East to d1, north to d2. Red builds east-side territory.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =SWS= -- South to c4, west to b4, south to b3. Blue spends bank immediately for aggressive west-side expansion.
- Turn 2 (Red): =nw= -- North to d3, west to c3. Red pushes into the center.
- Turn 2 (Blue): =SE= -- South to b2, east to c2. Blue expands south-center.
- Turn 3 (Red): =wn= -- West to b3 (capturing Blue territory), north to b4. Red steals Blue's west flank, reaching 6 territory.
- Turn 3 (Blue): =WS= -- West to b1, south? No -- west to a2, south to a1. Blue retreats to rebuild in the corner.
- Turn 4 (Red): =fx= -- Fortify b4. Red fort 1.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =WN= -- West to a1? Re-checking: Blue at b1, west a1, north a2. Blue builds a-column territory.
- Turn 5 (Red): =sf= -- South to b3, fortify. Red fort 2. Fort wall forming along b-column.
- Turn 5 (Blue): =FX= -- Fortify at current position. Blue fort 1.
- Turn 6 (Red): =sf= -- South to b2, fortify. Red fort 3. Wall extends: b4-b3-b2.
- Turn 6 (Blue): =NX= -- North, end. Blue repositions but is boxed in.
- Turn 7 (Red): =sf= -- South to b1, fortify. Red fort 4.
- Turn 7 (Blue): =NX= -- North, end. Blue cannot keep pace.
- Turn 8 (Red): =wf= -- West to a1, fortify. Red fort 5! Red wins.

** Key moment
Turn 3: Red's invasion of Blue's west flank (=wn= to b3-b4) was decisive. By capturing Blue territory, Red gained 6 squares for immediate fortification while denying Blue the base needed to compete.

** Lesson
Red's first-mover advantage enables aggressive territory theft. Expanding toward Blue's territory denies Blue the 5 squares needed for fortification.

* Game 63: Red wins in 8 turns

:WINNER: red
:TURNS: 8

** Play-by-play
- Turn 1 (Red): =en= -- East to d1, north to d2. Red starts building east column.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =WSE= -- West to b5, south to b4, east to c4. Blue spends bank for west-center expansion.
- Turn 2 (Red): =en= -- East to e2, north to e3. Red pushes deep east.
- Turn 2 (Blue): =SW= -- South to c3, west to b3. Blue builds west side.
- Turn 3 (Red): =nw= -- North to e4, west to d4. Red at 6 territory (c1, d1, d2, e2, e3, e4).
- Turn 3 (Blue): =SW= -- South to b2, west to a2. Blue reaches 7 territory.
- Turn 4 (Red): =fx= -- Fortify d4. Red fort 1.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =FX= -- Fortify a2. Blue fort 1.
- Turn 5 (Red): =ef= -- East to e4, fortify. Red fort 2.
- Turn 5 (Blue): =NF= -- North to a3, fortify. Blue fort 2.
- Turn 6 (Red): =nf= -- North to e5, fortify. Red fort 3.
- Turn 6 (Blue): =NF= -- North to a4, fortify. Blue fort 3.
- Turn 7 (Red): =wf= -- West to d5, fortify. Red fort 4.
- Turn 7 (Blue): =NF= -- North to a5, fortify. Blue fort 4.
- Turn 8 (Red): =wf= -- West to c5, fortify. Red fort 5! Red wins by one turn.

** Key moment
Both sides built parallel fort columns -- Red along row 4-5 sweeping west, Blue along column a going north. The race was perfectly symmetric except Red went first, winning by exactly one turn.

** Lesson
In a symmetric game, the first-mover advantage is decisive. Both sides reach 5 forts in 8 turns, but Red always arrives first. Blue needs an asymmetric technique (the double-fort) to overcome this.

* Game 66: Blue wins in 7 turns
:PROPERTIES:
:STEPS: en|WSX|en|SWX|nw|SEX|fx|FEF|ef|NF|nf|NF|wf|NF
:WINNER: blue
:TURNS: 7
:END:

** Play-by-play
- Turn 1 (Red): =en= -- East to d1, north to d2.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =WSX= -- West to b5, south to b4, end. Blue preserves bank=1.
- Turn 2 (Red): =en= -- East to e2, north to e3.
- Turn 2 (Blue): =SWX= -- South to b3, west to a3, end. Bank preserved.
- Turn 3 (Red): =nw= -- North to e4, west to d4. Red at 6 territory.
- Turn 3 (Blue): =SEX= -- South to a2, east to b2, end. Blue at 6 territory. Bank=1.
- Turn 4 (Red): =fx= -- Fortify d4. Red fort 1.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =FEF= -- DOUBLE FORT. Fortify b2, east to c2, fortify c2. Bank spent on the third action. Blue forts: 2!
- Turn 5 (Red): =ef= -- East to e4, fortify. Red fort 2.
- Turn 5 (Blue): =NF= -- North to c3, fortify. Blue fort 3.
- Turn 6 (Red): =nf= -- North to e5, fortify. Red fort 3.
- Turn 6 (Blue): =NF= -- North to c4, fortify. Blue fort 4.
- Turn 7 (Red): =wf= -- West to d5, fortify. Red fort 4.
- Turn 7 (Blue): =NF= -- North to c5, fortify. Blue fort 5! Blue wins!

** Key moment
Turn 4 Blue's =FEF= double-fort was the game-winning move. By placing 2 forts in one turn (spending the banked move on the third action), Blue gained a permanent 1-fort lead that Red could never recover.

** Lesson
The double-fort technique (FEF = fortify, east, fortify) is Blue's strongest use of the bank. It compensates for going second by creating a tempo advantage in the fort race.

* Game 67: Blue wins in 7 turns

:WINNER: blue
:TURNS: 7

** Play-by-play
- Turn 1 (Red): =wn= -- West to b1, north to b2. Red builds west side.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =ESX= -- East to d5, south to d4, end. Blue preserves bank. Blue builds east side.
- Turn 2 (Red): =nw= -- North to b3, west to a3. Red extends through a-b columns.
- Turn 2 (Blue): =SEX= -- South to d3, east to e3, end. Bank preserved.
- Turn 3 (Red): =ne= -- North to a4, east to b4. Red reaches 6 territory.
- Turn 3 (Blue): =SWX= -- South to e2, west to d2, end. Blue at 6 territory. Bank=1.
- Turn 4 (Red): =fx= -- Fortify b4. Red fort 1.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =FWF= -- DOUBLE FORT. Fortify d2, west to c2 (unclaimed), fortify c2. Bank spent. Blue forts: 2!
- Turn 5 (Red): =nf= -- North to b5, fortify. Red fort 2.
- Turn 5 (Blue): =NF= -- North to c3, fortify. Blue fort 3.
- Turn 6 (Red): =wf= -- West to a5, fortify. Red fort 3.
- Turn 6 (Blue): =NF= -- North to c4, fortify. Blue fort 4.
- Turn 7 (Red): =sf= -- South to a4, fortify. Red fort 4.
- Turn 7 (Blue): =NF= -- North to c5, fortify. Blue fort 5! Blue wins!

** Key moment
Turn 4 Blue's =FWF= double-fort is the westward variant: fortify, west, fortify. The key is that west leads to unclaimed territory (c2), preserving Blue's territory count for subsequent forts.

** Lesson
The double-fort works in any direction as long as the middle step goes to unclaimed or enemy territory (not Blue's own territory). FWF is the mirror image of FEF.

* Game 75: Red wins in 8 turns

- Turn 6 (Red): =nf= -- North to c5, fortify. Red fort 3. Red captures Blue's c5 territory!

The double-fort is not invincible. If Red forts INTO Blue's planned fort path instead of continuing east, Red can block Blue's expansion. The key is reading Blue's fort direction and planting a blocking fort. This is the strongest Red counter to FEF.

In the speed race, what matters is reaching 6 territory by Turn 3 and starting forts at Turn 4. The exact path is secondary to the timing. Red's first-mover advantage makes this a consistent win.

* Game 77: Blue wins in 7 turns

:TURNS: 7

** Play-by-play
- Turn 1 (Red): =ne= -- North to c2, east to d2. Red opens through the center.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =WSX= -- West to b5, south to b4, end. Bank preserved.
- Turn 2 (Red): =nw= -- North to d3, west to c3. Red pushes into the center-west.
- Turn 2 (Blue): =SWX= -- South to b3, west to a3, end. Bank preserved.
- Turn 3 (Red): =en= -- East to d3 (own territory), north to d4. Red at d4 with 5 territory (c1, c2, d2, d3, c3).

- Turn 4 (Red): =fx= -- Fortify d4. Red fort 1.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =FEF= -- Double fort: fortify b2, east to c2 (Red territory -- captured!), fortify c2. Blue forts: 2!
- Turn 5 (Red): =en= -- East to e4, north to e5. Red's squareCount is only 4 (c1, d2, d3) -- c2 and c3 were captured by Blue's FEF. Red cannot fortify!

** Key moment

** Lesson
Red's central opening (=ne nw en=) through c2 is vulnerable to FEF because Blue's eastward step in FEF captures c2. Red loses territory AND tempo. The safer Red openings go through d1 (via =en=), keeping territory away from Blue's FEF capture square.

* Game 80: Red wins in 8 turns
:PROPERTIES:
:STEPS: en|WSX|ne|SWX|nw|SEX|fx|FEF|wf|NF|nf|WN|ef|WX|ef
:WINNER: red
:TURNS: 8
:END:

** Play-by-play
- Turn 1 (Red): =en= -- East to d1, north to d2.
- Turn 1 (Blue): =WSX= -- West to b5, south to b4, end. Bank preserved.
- Turn 2 (Red): =ne= -- North to d3, east to e3.
- Turn 2 (Blue): =SWX= -- South to b3, west to a3, end. Bank preserved.
- Turn 3 (Red): =nw= -- North to e4, west to d4. Red at 6 territory.
- Turn 3 (Blue): =SEX= -- South to a2, east to b2, end. Blue at 6 territory. Bank=1.
- Turn 4 (Red): =fx= -- Fortify d4. Red fort 1.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =FEF= -- Double fort: b2 and c2. Blue takes the fort lead with 2 forts.
- Turn 5 (Red): =wf= -- West to c4, fortify! Red blocks Blue's fort column at c4. Red fort 2.
- Turn 5 (Blue): =NF= -- North to c3, fortify. Blue fort 3.
- Turn 6 (Red): =nf= -- North to c5, fortify. Red captures c5 from Blue. Red fort 3.
- Turn 6 (Blue): =WN= -- West to b3, north to b4. Blue detours around Red's c4 blocking fort.
- Turn 7 (Red): =ef= -- East to d5, fortify. Red fort 4.
- Turn 7 (Blue): =WX= -- West to a4, end. Blue's territory count is 4 -- below the threshold for fortification. Blue is stuck at 3 forts.
- Turn 8 (Red): =ef= -- East to e5, fortify. Red fort 5! Red wins!

** Key moment
Turn 5 Red's =wf= to c4 was the key counter. Instead of continuing east (the standard plan), Red planted a fort directly in Blue's planned fort column. This forced Blue to detour, costing a tempo that negated the double-fort advantage. By Turn 6, Red captured c5, permanently reducing Blue's territory below the fortification threshold.

** Lesson
The double-fort is not unbeatable. Red's counter-strategy is to recognize Blue's fort direction and block it with a fort of their own. The c4 blocking fort is a concrete refutation of the standard FEF-northward plan. This means Blue must either vary the fort direction or Red will always have a counter.
