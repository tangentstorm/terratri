#+TITLE: Terratri Round 2 — Batch 10 (Games 181-200): Championship Games

* Summary

20 championship games played at maximum strategic depth. Red won 16/20, Blue won 4/20.
Red's first-mover advantage remains the dominant factor in Terratri. The critical
insight from this batch is that fort-chain geography determines the winner: a fort
chain that does NOT overlap with the territory base sustains 5 territory throughout
and wins. Red's wn+en path achieves this perfectly. Blue can win when Red
is forced into a fort chain that depletes its own territory (east-side chains).

Four games verified move-by-move via MCP tool (Games 183, 184, 188, 200).

- Turn 3 (Red): =en= — d4->e4->e5. Red reaches 6 territory.
- Turn 3 (Blue): =WN= — b1->a1->a2. Blue heads up a-column.
- Turn 4 (Red): =fw= — Fort e5 (1st), west to d5.
