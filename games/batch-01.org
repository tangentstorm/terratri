** Play-by-play

- Turn 4 (Red): =ww= — Red retreats westward: d1 to c1 to b1, trying to expand the west flank.
- Turn 4 (Blue): =FK= — Blue fortifies at e1 and banks a move. Smart defensive play, building fort supply while saving tempo.

- Turn 6 (Red): =nf= — Red moves north to b3 and fortifies. Three forts, but Blue is ahead.
- Turn 6 (Blue): =NF= — Blue marches north to d3 and fortifies. Four forts, one away from victory.
- Turn 7 (Red): =ne= — Red desperately moves north to b4 then east to c4, but cannot fortify.

** Key moment
Turn 4 was decisive. Blue's decision to fortify and bank (=FK=) gave her a tempo advantage, while Red's westward retreat (=ww=) wasted moves without fortifying. Blue then used the banked move in Turn 5 to invade Red's center territory and fortify, seizing the initiative permanently.

** Lesson
Edge sweeping combined with banking can outpace center rushing. The first-move advantage for Red is offset by Blue's initial banked move, especially when Blue uses it to build fort tempo.
